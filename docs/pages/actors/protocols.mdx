# Protocols

A Silo lets solvers sell fee tokens for one resource token and send the output to a fixed recipient. Choose a treasury or Burner as the recipient. Before sending inventory, confirm that the intended CoW integration supports your Silo. Use direct ERC-20 transfers or configure a funding adapter. Do not send the resource token to the Silo: the Silo cannot sell it.

## Choose the required output

Set `initialBuyAmount`, `minimumBuyAmount` (the floor), and `auctionDuration` at deployment. The amounts are in the resource token's smallest unit and apply to the **whole balance of one sell token**. They do not specify a price per unit. A deposit can double the amount for sale without changing the required output.

For a flat floor, set the two amounts equal. For a falling auction, set the initial amount above the nonzero floor and choose a positive duration in seconds. The clock starts at deployment, even for a token the Silo has never held. No token registration or keeper is needed. Use `currentMinimumBuyAmount(sellToken)` to read the required output for that token.

During a falling auction, the Silo subtracts `floor((initialBuyAmount - minimumBuyAmount) * elapsed / auctionDuration)` from `initialBuyAmount`. Here `elapsed` is seconds since deployment or this token's last full sale. At or after `auctionDuration`, the required output stays at `minimumBuyAmount`.

For example, with an initial output of 150 USDC, a floor of 100 USDC, and a duration of two hours, the required output is 125 USDC after one hour. After two hours it stays at 100 USDC until a full sale. If token A then sells, new inventory of A starts at 150 USDC at the sale timestamp. Token B stays at 100 USDC if it has not sold. A deposit, funding call, or commit without a fill does not restart either clock.

These settings are immutable. To change them, deploy a new Silo and direct future inventory to it. Monitor Settlement fills and resource-token transfers to the recipient. An `OrderCommitted` event alone does not confirm a sale.

## Optional source funding

A new Silo has no adapter. To use approved source inventory, deploy `ApprovalFundingAdapter(source, silo)` with nonzero addresses. The Silo owner calls `setFundingAdapter(adapter)`. The source grants each sell-token allowance to the adapter, not to the Silo or Vault Relayer.

Only the configured Silo can call `fund(token)`. The adapter transfers the smaller of the source balance and its allowance. A successful commit can move inventory into the Silo even if no trade follows. Use this approval only if the source can release inventory before a sale. If the settlement transaction reverts, the transfer also reverts.

The owner can replace the adapter or disable it with `setFundingAdapter(address(0))`. Revoke the old adapter's allowances separately. A custom adapter must use the source protocol's existing permissions.
